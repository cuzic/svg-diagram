## Findings

### M1 (Major): the half of the new check that catches chip-on-line has no test, and removing it brings back silent overlaps
- Evidence: in a scratch copy, I changed lib/verify-connect-geometry.ts:39 from `[o.labelBox, ...o.pathSegments]` to `[o.labelBox]`, so that only chip-on-chip is checked. test/swimlane*.test.ts and test/id-scope*.test.ts pass (54/54). The full suite gives the same failures as the unmutated copy (see Other checks). With this mutation, the fuzz (seed 1, 500 figures) goes from 0 silent and 111 reported to 94 silent and 17 reported. The round-3 test "a chip that has no free place…" only puts a chip on a chip. Removing the whole check (`if (false)`) does fail that test.
- Fix: add a no-room case where the way-back chip has to cover another flow's vertical leg (not its chip), and assert the `label overlaps connect(...)` problem.

### M2 (Major): a labelled hand-over from a middle box puts its chip on the way back's line, and `check` passes
- Evidence: lanes `b` (`s01`..`s05`, chained) and `c` (`o13` at column 3), plus `{from: 's05', to: 's02', label: 'Go back'}` and `{from: 's03', to: 'o13', label: 'Pass'}`. The way back runs at y=110 from x=380 to 843. The hand-over's chip `[511,104,45,24]` (labelAt 0.4 on its leg x=534, from 98 to 144) is cut through by that line. `problems` is empty. The only warning is the `edge-crossings` warning about the lines crossing, which this figure cannot avoid. The new check (lib/verify-connect-geometry.ts:39) looks only from the way back's chip outward. The swimlane layout gives the hand-over's `labelAt` without looking at the runs under the row. In the fuzz (seed 1, 500 figures), another flow's chip lies on a way back's line 171 times, and 61 of those figures have `problems` empty. The same output appears at c44ad879 and 5105ea06, so this has been there since the runs were added, not caused by 99aa4141. But it is the same kind of silent chip-on-line that M2 of round 3 was about, and the input is a common one: a hand-over from a box that a way back passes under.
- Fix: in the check, also report any connect's `labelBox` that overlaps an `under` connect's path segments. In the layout, give a labelled hand-over that crosses the runs a `labelAt` that puts its chip below the deepest run of the row (or above the run when it comes in from below). Add the case above as a test.

### m1 (Minor): parts of the new leg model are untested; mutating them makes more chips overlap (reported, so not silent), and no test fails
- Evidence: each of these mutations passes test/swimlane*.test.ts and test/id-scope*.test.ts. (a) Not counting the deeper ways back as legs (`o.group === u.group && o.off > u.off` → `false`): the fuzz goes from 111 to 321 reported overlaps. (b) Going back to the centre when nothing is free (`best < Infinity` → `best === 0`): 111 to 183 reported. (c) The `right` end at the centre (`st.x + st.w` → `st.x + st.w / 2`). (d) No ±13 elbow offset. (e) `o.off > u.off` → `true`. The model is also still approximate for flows with side ends. `s3:right -> c4:left` adds a leg at s3's right edge (x=597), but the line's vertical part is at x=612, and this moves a lone chip 6 px off the centre. An elbow's bend is at `bendOffset` 6/13/20, not always 13. A shared side's slot (`@0.80`) is modelled at the box centre. The reported-overlap check keeps all of this safe.
- Fix: add one test each for (a) and (b): a nested pair where the shallow chip must avoid the deep pair's legs, and a no-room case that asserts the chip is not at the centre. Optionally, use the slotted end x.

### m2 (Minor): the new problem does not name the label and does not say how to fix it
- Evidence: `connect(s13:bottom@0.33 -> s11:bottom@0.67) label overlaps connect(o12:top@0.67 -> s02:bottom@0.67)`. The other label messages in the same file give the label text and the size of the overlap. Writing `labelAt` on the way back moves the chip, but the message does not mention it.
- Fix: include `"<label>"` and the overlap in px, and add "(write labelAt on the flow, or shorten the label)".
